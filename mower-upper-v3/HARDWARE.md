# Hardware for upper housing V3

All sizes are metric unless explicitly marked otherwise. Screw length is measured from under the head. Reuse the existing leg, electronics and antenna hardware where already assembled.

| Connection | Hardware | Quantity |
|---|---|---:|
| Hood to base, front lower rail | M3 × 12 button-head machine screw + ordinary M3 hex nut | 2 of each |
| One-piece face to hood, upper outer corners | M3 × 12 button-head machine screw + ordinary M3 hex nut | 2 of each |
| Base to original three legs | M5 × 30 low button-head screw + M5 washer; original nuts in legs | 3 |
| Electronics carriers to base | M3 × 16 machine screw, M3 nut and washer | 8 of each |
| Two antenna beams to base | M4 × 30 machine screw, M4 nut and washer | 4 of each |
| Antenna plate to beams | M4 × 35 machine screw, M4 nut and washer | 4 of each |
| Internal LCD retaining rings | 3 × 8 thread-forming screw **for plastic** | 4 |
| Camera cassette to front face | 3 × 8 thread-forming screw **for plastic** | 2 |
| AI Camera PCB to cassette | 2 × 5 thread-forming screw **for plastic** | 4 |
| Pi to printed spacers/carrier, provisional | M2.5 × 14 machine screw, M2.5 nut, broad washer OD ≥7 mm | 4 of each |

The only new cover hardware is **4 M3 × 12 button-head screws and 4 ordinary M3 hex nuts**. A small M3 assortment containing this exact length is sufficient for those connections. Ordinary M3 nuts are approximately 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick; the nominal pockets are 5.8 mm across flats. Tall nyloc nuts do not fit these pockets. Fit-test actual nuts in PETG before the full prints.

The face screws are sized without added washers. Hood screw washers are optional and must not reduce engagement below the full nut thickness. Use low button heads for the base-to-leg fasteners to clear the electronics carriers. Do not tighten until PETG deforms.

LCD and camera screws form their thread directly in plastic; a metric machine-screw assortment does not automatically supply these. The camera screw must be diameter **2.0 mm**, to pass the PCB's Ø2.2 holes; American #2 is a different size. Do not replace the short camera screws with long ones without checking component clearance.

For Harxon GPS1000 use a **metal BSW 5/8-inch, 11 TPI** bolt, stud or mounting adapter. The printed plate has a Ø16.5 mm clearance hole, not a thread. Preserve the antenna maker's 12–14 mm engagement, and choose the metal stack and locking arrangement to suit the actual antenna underside. M16 is not a substitute.

The old V2 floor joiner M4 × 16 fasteners, roof joiner M3 × 16 fasteners and four plastic-thread face-to-hood screws are eliminated. Existing V1 clamp and leg hardware stays as previously specified. No hardware has been ordered.
