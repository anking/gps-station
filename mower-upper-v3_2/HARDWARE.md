The supplied revised eye mount has 1.2 mm pilot holes and stepped/countersunk details; the retaining rings have 3.4 mm clearance holes. These dimensions are copied from your STEP files. The earlier generic 3 x 8 eye-ring screw recommendation must not be used without checking that it matches the revised pilot and head/washer arrangement.

The dome has four blind 2 mm pilots, 10 mm deep. Four 2.5 x 8 plastic-thread-forming screws are a starting point to test in TEST_dome_screw. With a 3.5 mm roof and a thin washer, engagement is approximately 4-4.5 mm. Keep the original slightly offset 48 x 48 mm pattern: X=-23.8/+24.2, Y=-24.2/+23.8. The hood holes are 2.7 mm clearance. No dome nuts or heat-set inserts are specified.

Use 2 mm round elastomer O-ring cord in the 2.6 x 1.5 mm underside groove. Groove pitch diameter is 78 mm, centreline circumference about 245 mm. Cut/trim and splice for the actual cord and verify compression physically.

For the four external cover screws use ordinary M3 nuts about 5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick. Tall nyloc nuts do not fit the shallow pockets. Use low heads; the upper visor screws are sized without added washers. The lower 7.2 mm visor openings admit the recessed hood screw heads and driver.

Keep the existing metal BSW 5/8-inch-11 antenna mounting hardware and the antenna maker's 12-14 mm thread engagement. The printed antenna plate is a clearance hole, not a printed thread. M16 is not equivalent.

The obsolete electronics platforms, spacers and eight tray fasteners are removed. Hardware for electronics mounted directly to the base depends on the final component layout and is not preselected. Tighten plastic parts by hand without crushing them. No hardware has been ordered.
